About the board

A free, independent happy-hour board for the western suburbs. Every window is transcribed from a source linked on its own card, with the date it was last checked shown in the open. Around four in five bars never publish a happy hour anywhere, so every licensed bar, restaurant and brewery gets a card either way — the ones we can't prove say so, and ask you to fill them in.
