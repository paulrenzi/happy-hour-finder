About the board

A free, independent happy-hour board for the western suburbs. Every window is transcribed from a source linked on its own card, and the date it was last checked is on the venue's own page. Around four in five bars never publish a happy hour anywhere, so every licensed bar, restaurant and brewery gets a card either way — the ones we can't prove say so, and ask you to fill them in.

New happy hours and nights out, by email

When a bar near you publishes a happy hour we hadn't seen, or a band, trivia night or show goes on the board. A short note, not a newsletter. Nothing is sent until you confirm.
